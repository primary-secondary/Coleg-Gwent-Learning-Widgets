Unit 2: Fundamentals of Computer Systems

Inside the Computer: Main Internal Components

Identify the main components inside a computer, understand what each one does, and see how they work together.

Work one section at a time. Use Check at the end of each part.

Correct answers lock in. Incorrect answers are not revealed. Some questions contain clues that may help with later questions — use them.

Answer matching: capital letters do not matter. Common abbreviations, full names, alternative valid terms, spacing, punctuation and singular/plural forms are accepted.

Part A

What am I?

Start by identifying the main internal components from their jobs.

1.
I execute instructions, perform calculations and control much of what the computer does. What am I?
2.
I temporarily hold programs and data that are currently being used. My contents are lost when the power is switched off. What am I?
3.
I am non-volatile memory used to hold firmware or startup instructions. In basic computer-system diagrams I am often labelled ______.
4.
I provide long-term storage using spinning magnetic platters. What am I?
5.
I provide long-term storage using flash memory and have no moving parts. What am I?
6.
I am the main circuit board that components such as the CPU and RAM connect to. What am I?
7.
I am a set of electrical pathways carrying data, addresses or control signals between components. What am I?
